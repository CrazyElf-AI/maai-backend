What We Do

Our Activities

From ground-level camps to podcasts — every activity is a step toward a healthier community.

Community Health Camps

Bringing healthcare directly to communities — urban and rural alike.

Health Check-ups

Free basic health screenings including BP, sugar, BMI and general consultation for underserved populations.

Screening Initiatives

Anemia checks, vision screening, dental checks, and early disease detection programs at scale.

Rural & Urban Outreach

Taking camps to remote villages and urban slums where access to healthcare is limited or unavailable.

Preventive Counseling

One-on-one sessions to guide individuals on lifestyle choices, diet, and preventive health measures.

Awareness Sessions

Education that empowers — from schools to streets.

School & College Programs

Menstrual health education, puberty guidance, and reproductive health awareness for students in a safe, empathetic environment.

Nutrition & Hygiene Workshops

Practical workshops on balanced diets, hand hygiene, sanitation, and lifestyle disease awareness for all age groups.

Basic First Aid & Emergency

Teaching communities how to respond to medical emergencies — CPR, wound care, choking response, and when to call for help.

Oral Health & Caries Prevention

Spreading awareness about dental hygiene, cavity prevention, regular check-ups, and fluoride use among children and adults.

Our Team Force

The backbone of every camp — people who show up and make it happen.

Core Student Leadership

Students driving strategy, operations, and vision across all MAAI programmes.

Medical Volunteers

Doctors, nurses, and medical students providing clinical expertise at every camp.

Field Coordinators

On-the-ground leaders managing venue setup, patient flow, and logistics in real time.

Program Directors

Senior members overseeing specific verticals — camps, awareness, partnerships, and content.

Leadership Training & Exposure

Building the next generation of community health leaders.

Structured Leadership Roles

Providing real leadership responsibilities within committees and projects, building accountability from day one.

Youth Mentoring

Mentoring youth in project planning, team coordination, and decision-making to become effective community leaders.

Collaborative Learning

Promoting peer-led discussions, cross-team collaboration, and shared learning to foster growth across all levels.

School & College Camps

Healthy students, stronger futures.

Health Check-up Camps

Organizing basic check-ups — BMI, malnutrition assessment, anemia and vision screening within school and college campuses.

Mental Well-being Sessions

Facilitating discussions on stress management, exam anxiety, and emotional well-being tailored for students.

Healthy Habits

Encouraging physical activity, balanced nutrition, hygiene practices, and vaccination awareness among young students.

Skill Development Workshops

Building capable, confident health volunteers from the ground up.

Camp Organization Skills

Workshops on health camp organization, screening protocols, and epidemiological basics to run efficient camps.

Communication & Soft Skills

Sessions on patient communication, public speaking, professional etiquette, and community engagement skills.

BLS & Emergency Preparedness

Basic Life Support awareness, triage principles, and disaster/emergency preparedness training for all volunteers.

MAAI Podcast

Tuning into voices that drive change.

Expert Conversations

Engaging conversations with healthcare professionals and industry experts sharing insights on public health and preventive care.

Public Health Trends

Discussing emerging trends in community health, preventive medicine, and the real-world impact of grassroots organisations.

Volunteer Stories

Inspiring stories from volunteers and organizers sharing their on-ground experiences, challenges, and moments of impact.

Actionable Advice

Broadcasting health advice and organisational leadership tips to empower a wider audience beyond our immediate reach.
