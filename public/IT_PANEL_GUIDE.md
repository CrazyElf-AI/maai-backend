# MAAI IT Panel User Guide

Welcome to the IT Panel! This guide will help you navigate and use the content management features available to your role.

## 🌟 Overview
The IT Panel is a dedicated workspace for IT and content management staff. It allows you to update website media, easily view team members, and keep content fresh, without accidentally changing critical website settings.

---

## 🛠️ What You Can Do

### 1. 🖼️ Media Content Manager
Keep the website visually engaging.
*   **Gallery Images**: Upload new photos from recent camps and events. You can also delete outdated images.
*   **Camp Reels**: Add YouTube/Vimeo embed links for new camp videos.
*   **Testimonials**: Share inspiring stories by adding quotes, names, roles, and photos of volunteers or partners.

### 2. 👥 Team Management
Manage our growing family of volunteers.
*   **Add Members**: Quickly add new members using their name and email.
*   **View & Search**: Look up existing members and check when they joined.
*   *Note: IT staff cannot change member roles (like making someone an Admin) or delete members. That is handled by Administrators.*

### 3. 👔 Leadership Management
Keep the leadership roster up to date.
*   **Add/Edit Profiles**: Update names, roles, bios, categories (e.g., Founding Members, Mentors), and social media links.
*   **Remove Profiles**: Delete profiles if someone steps down.

### 4. ⭐ Flagship Initiatives
Showcase MAAI's primary projects.
*   **Add Initiatives**: Create new initiative cards with titles, categories, descriptions, and images.
*   **Remove Initiatives**: Clean up old or completed projects.

### 5. 💼 Careers Management
Help recruit new talent.
*   **Post Jobs**: Add new career openings with titles, types (e.g., Full-time, Remote), descriptions, and application links.
*   **Remove Postings**: Take down filled positions.

---

## 🚫 What is Restricted?
To ensure system stability, the following features are restricted and require **Admin** or **God Mode** access:
*   **Core Settings**: Changing global website configurations or registration fees.
*   **Role Assignment**: Changing a user's role to IT, Admin, or God.
*   **Advanced User Management**: Permanently deleting users or approving/rejecting NGO camp requests.
*   **System Notifications**: Broadcasting global messages to all users.

---

## 🔑 How to Access the IT Panel
1.  Go to the main **Login Page** (`/login.html`).
2.  Log in using your registered email and password.
3.  Once authenticated, the system will verify your role securely via Firebase.
4.  If your account has the **`it`**, **`admin`**, or **`god`** role, you will see a link to the IT Panel in the sidebar of your Member Portal, or you can navigate directly to `/it-panel.html`.

*If you believe you should have access but see an "Access Denied" message, please contact a System Administrator to update your permissions.*
